01 — SWR eFinance Marketing & Growth Specialist · Jan to Sep 2026

One journey,
two systems

A live financing platform where customers apply online and agents work the case through to disbursement. I designed the customer journey, the workflow logic and the front-end experience for both sides, then worked with the technical team to bring it into production.

Lead Generation Customer Journey Digital Workflow Customer & Agent Portals Sales & Ops Coordination

Key result One shared pipeline replacing separate spreadsheets for customers, agents and sales

The Challenge

Cases were tracked across spreadsheets and chat threads. Nobody could tell where one was stuck, customers could not check progress, and agents had no shared view of the day's work.

My Role

Marketing & Growth Specialist. I owned the customer journey, the workflow logic and the front end experience for both portals, working with the back end team to turn that into a working system.

What I Did
  • Mapped the 7-stage pipeline from Semakan Awal to Disbursed with sales and operations.
  • Designed the customer journey: pre-check form, guided document upload, self-serve tracker.
  • Designed the agent portal: case queue, document checklist, offer calculator, activity history.
  • Built the front end for both portals and wrote the brief the technical team built from.
  • Tracked where enquiries stalled — form, documents or approval — to know what to fix next.
Result
  • One shared pipeline for customers, agents and sales instead of separate spreadsheets.
  • Customers track their own application, so fewer calls just to ask for an update.
  • Every case has a documented next step and a full activity history.

Xanderia Marketing

Digital Marketing Executive · Oct to Dec 2025

Paid social lead generation for personal financing products on Meta and TikTok over three months. I also reviewed the client's own disbursement records and rebuilt the monthly figures the team was reporting.

What I Did

Planned the campaign structure, targeting and budget. Wrote and iterated ad copy, directed the creative, and optimised while campaigns were live.

Applications Submitted

RM5.5M across 272 applications

Converted

RM2.8M disbursed · 142 loans · 52.2%

Ad Spend

RM51,627 across Meta and TikTok

Objective

Generate qualified financing enquiries from salaried government and GLC employees, at a cost per lead the sales team could work with. Audiences were split by state so budget followed demand. Run on Meta and TikTok.

What I Did
  • Planned the campaign structure, the targeting and the budget allocation.
  • Split audiences by state so spend moved to where leads actually converted.
  • Wrote and iterated the ad copy, directed the creative, and matched the message to each segment.
  • Managed budgets and pacing daily, refreshing creative when performance started to drop.
  • Tracked cost per lead with the sales team, treating a rising CPL as a signal to change something.
Result

Campaigns ran across Meta and TikTok over three months, working alongside the sales team through to approval. Of the RM5.5M applied for, RM2.8M was disbursed across 142 loans; the rest was rejected or cancelled. The two figures stay separate here because marketing generates the applications and the lender decides what pays out.
